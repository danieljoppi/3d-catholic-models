# 0003. Provenance recorded before distribution

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-09-06

## Context

The purpose of this repository is to put models in other people's hands —
individuals and small parishes. Devotional art is old but not therefore public
domain: a photograph of a 19th-century carving carries the photographer's
rights, and a living sculptor's work carries theirs.

Set 01's panels turned out to be generated by the repository owner with Google
Gemini Pro, which is a different situation again. Future sets may come from
photographs, commissions, or other artists.

## Decision

Every set's `metadata.json` carries a `rights` block naming the author, the
licence, and whether a third party is involved. It is filled in before anything
derived from that set is published or shared.

Where origins differ within a set, the exception is recorded on the piece.

## Consequences

Nothing ships on an assumption about its origin. The block is also the place
where the awkward facts get written down rather than rediscovered — for set 01,
that purely AI-generated images are generally not copyrightable in the US and
several other jurisdictions (the models sculpted from them are original work
and protected normally), and that Gemini output carries SynthID watermarking
which survives in the JPEGs.

The cost is a `TODO` sitting in metadata until someone answers it. That is the
point: it is visible.

## Alternatives considered

**Record it at publication time.** By then the reference's origin has usually
been forgotten, which is exactly when it matters.

**A single repository-wide licence.** Cannot work — origins genuinely differ
per set, and one statement would be wrong for some of them.
