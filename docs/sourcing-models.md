# Sourcing models from the community

Chapel Anywhere does not have to sculpt everything. Much of what furnishes a
chapel has already been modelled well and given away, and the fastest route to
a furnished prayer space runs through other people's work as well as ours.

This page is the curated route. **Check it before modelling anything** — a
worse copy of a good free crucifix is not a contribution.

The reasoning, and the rule that governs all of it, is
[ADR-0011](adr/0011-link-to-community-models-never-vendor-them.md).

---

## The one rule

> **Read the licence on the model's own page, every time you download.**

Not the platform's general terms. Not what a sibling model by the same designer
said. Not what this page said on the date below.

Licences on these platforms are chosen per upload and can be changed later by
the author, and the range runs from CC BY — do what you like with attribution —
to MakerWorld's Standard Digital File License, which permits personal printing
only, with **no file redistribution and no remixes**. Creative Commons `NC`
forbids selling what you print; `ND` forbids distributing anything you modify.

For a parish printing for its own use, most terms are fine. The moment money,
redistribution or a remix is involved, they stop being fine, and the difference
lives on the model page.

**Nothing third-party is copied into this repository.** Downloads go to your
slicer. Everything in `collections/` is ours, which is the only reason
[ADR-0003](adr/0003-provenance-before-distribution.md) can promise anything
about provenance.

## Where the community is strong, and where it is not

Surveyed 2026-09-07 across MakerWorld and Printables.

| Mission area | State | What that means for us |
|---|---|---|
| Sacred statuary | **Strong** | Link. Marian figures especially |
| Crucifixes | **Strong** | Link |
| Holy water fonts | **Adequate** | Link; a dedicated account covers most of it |
| Candle holders | **Decorative only** | Usable, but cathedral-*inspired* ornaments rather than liturgical furniture |
| The Liturgical Heart | **Thin** | Altar frontals, ambons and tabernacle adornments are mostly dollhouse miniatures. **Ours to make** |
| Stations of the Cross | **Absent** | No complete set found anywhere. **Ours to make** — and the reason set 01 exists |

That is the whole strategy in one table. The community has the devotional
figures; nobody has built the sanctuary at real scale, and nobody has published
a coherent Via Crucis.

## Adding to this page

Anyone can. Keep it to what earns a place in a chapel:

- **Link, never a file.** No STL from another site enters this repository.
- **Say what it is, in one line** — what makes it worth printing, or the catch.
- **Say nothing about the licence.** Not even "free". It can change, and a
  stale licence note here is worse than none: it invites someone to skip the
  check that ADR-0011 exists to make them do.
- **Note the iconography** if a piece is easily mistaken for another saint.
- **Prefer a collection or tag page** to a deep link when both would serve.

If a link here is dead, delete it. A rotting catalogue is worse than a short
one, and nothing automated will ever tell us — both platforms return `403` to
scripts, so this page is maintained by people reading it or not at all.
