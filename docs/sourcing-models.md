# Free models worth printing

A list of free, downloadable 3D models that help furnish a chapel, with links
straight to them. Nothing here is downloaded or stored in this repository — it
is a map of what already exists, so we only sculpt what nobody has.

Surveyed 2026-09-07 across MakerWorld, Printables, Thingiverse and Cults3D. All
models listed were free to download at that time.

**Before you print one, read the licence on its own page.** Licences are set
per model by the person who uploaded it, and they range from "do anything with
attribution" to "print it yourself and nothing else". For a parish printing for
its own use almost all of them are fine; selling prints or sharing modified
files is where they differ. Reasoning in
[ADR-0011](adr/0011-link-to-community-models-never-vendor-them.md).

## Where the gaps are

| Mission area | Community | |
|---|---|---|
| Sacred statuary | **Strong** | Print theirs |
| Crucifixes and crosses | **Strong** | Print theirs |
| Holy water fonts | **Good** | Print theirs |
| Nativity | **Strong** | Print theirs |
| Rosary and devotional objects | **Good** | Print theirs |
| Candle holders | **Decorative** | Cathedral-*styled* ornaments, not liturgical furniture |
| The Liturgical Heart | **Only on Cults3D** | MakerWorld and Printables have nothing but dollhouse miniatures. Cults3D has tabernacles, a handful of monstrances, and lecterns/ambos — worth assessing before we model our own |
| **Stations of the Cross** | **Empty** | No complete printable set anywhere. **Ours to make** — the reason set 01 exists |

The community has the devotional figures thoroughly covered. The sanctuary is
patchier and lives almost entirely on Cults3D. A coherent Via Crucis does not
exist — the nearest thing found was a single CNC relief of the twelfth station.

## The Liturgical Heart

The thinnest area, and the one worth assessing carefully before we commit to
modelling our own. Almost all of it is on Cults3D; MakerWorld and Printables
have essentially nothing at real scale.

| | Where | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Lectern / Ambo](https://cults3d.com/en/3d-model/tool/lectern-ambo-ambo-lectern) | Cults3D | A specific ambo model rather than a tag page |
| [tabernacle](https://cults3d.com/en/tags/tabernacle) | Cults3D | Around thirty free at survey time — mixed purpose, see the trap below |
| [monstrance](https://cults3d.com/en/tags/monstrance) | Cults3D | Only a handful free, and one of them is a cookie cutter |
| [lectern](https://cults3d.com/en/tags/lectern) · [altar (free)](https://cults3d.com/en/tags/altar?only_free=true) · [church-furniture](https://cults3d.com/en/tags/church-furniture) | Cults3D | Browse; much of it is secular or scale-model furniture |
| [Church furniture](https://www.cgtrader.com/3d-print-models/church-furniture) | CGTrader | Mostly paid, some free. Worth a look, not a first stop |

**Two traps.** "Tabernacle" mostly returns the *Tabernacle of Moses* — the
Exodus 25 tent, a Bible-study model, not a Eucharistic tabernacle. And the
sanctuary results skew heavily to dollhouse and wargame scale; check the
dimensions before assuming a piece can be printed at size.

This is the row where our own work is most likely to be worth doing. Assess
what is there first.

## Adding to this list

Anyone can. Keep it to what earns a place in a chapel:

- **A link, never a file.** No STL from another site enters this repository.
- **One line on what it is** — what makes it worth printing, or the catch.
- **Say nothing about the licence.** It can change, and a stale note here is
  worse than none: it invites someone to skip the check.
- **Note the iconography** if a piece is easily mistaken for another saint.
- **Delete dead links.** Nothing automated will find them — both MakerWorld and
  Printables refuse programmatic requests, so this page is maintained by people
  reading it or not at all.
